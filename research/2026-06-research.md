# SHOAL Research: June 2026 Landscape

**Status:** Initial research pass. Will go stale. Verify numbers before publishing.

**Compiled:** 2026-06-07 via Claude (Opus 4.6) deep research

## Summary

Full research report covering: hardware builds ($135 Raspberry Pi to $3,500 Mac Studio), Ollama + Open WebUI as the de facto stack, Open WebUI multi-user RBAC, historical parallels (Plex success vs. Windows Home Server/SBS failure), data sovereignty drivers (ABA Opinion 512 for legal, HIPAA for medical), cost comparison vs. ChatGPT Plus/Go/Business seats, and the content gap nobody is filling.

## Key Findings

1. No settled name for the pattern. "Home AI server" is the most search-indexed term.
2. Hardware floor: $800 Mac Mini M4 is the sweet spot. $200-450 refurb OptiPlex is the budget entry.
3. Ollama + Open WebUI is the stack. Open WebUI has mature multi-user RBAC.
4. Real concurrency: 2-6 light users on a single Mac Mini M4. 4-10 on M4 Pro.
5. Plex is the correct historical analogy. WHS/SBS are the cautionary tales.
6. Biggest content gap: no end-to-end "buy this, install this, share it" guide exists.
7. The cost case is real but the privacy case is stronger.
